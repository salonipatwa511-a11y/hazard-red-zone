DATA & METHODOLOGY

Data Sources & Assessment Methodology

The system combines government-sourced demographic and hazard information with a proposed project assessment model.

01

Census of India 2011

District-level population data is taken from the Census of India 2011 to represent the population exposed to potential hazards.

Government Source
02

Government of Bihar

Flood vulnerability classification of Bihar districts is based on government-published disaster vulnerability information.

Government Source
03

Risk Assessment Model

The proposed model combines hazard severity, vulnerability and carrying-capacity pressure to calculate a project risk score.

Project Model

Proposed Risk Calculation

Risk Score = (Hazard × 55%) + (Vulnerability × 25%) + (Capacity Pressure × 20%)
Hazard Severity

Represents the severity of the identified hazard.

Vulnerability

Represents the relative vulnerability of the affected population.

Carrying Capacity

Represents the estimated capacity available for supporting the affected population.

Relocation Priority

The calculated risk and capacity pressure are used to classify areas into relocation priority levels.

Important: Population and hazard classification are based on government sources. Risk score, carrying capacity and relocation priority are calculated outputs of the proposed project model and are not official government scores.
ABOUT THE PROJECT

Intelligent Disaster Risk Management System

A data-driven system designed to identify hazard-prone red zones, assess carrying-capacity pressure and support immediate relocation decisions for vulnerable communities.

🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to identify high-risk areas by combining population information, hazard severity, vulnerability and carrying-capacity indicators.

⚙️ How the System Works

The system processes district-level data and applies a proposed risk assessment model. Based on the calculated risk and capacity pressure, areas are assigned relocation priorities.

🚨 Key Features

  • Hazard-based red zone identification
  • Population assessment
  • Carrying-capacity analysis
  • Risk score calculation
  • Relocation priority classification

💡 Expected Impact

The system can help decision-makers understand which areas require greater attention and support faster, data-informed disaster management planning.
